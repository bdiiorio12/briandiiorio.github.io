A Cody & Koda Story

Meet the Family

The Briggs family farm, and everyone who calls it home.

Age 9 · The Notebook Keeper

Cody Briggs

Nine years old, farm-raised, and stubborn in the best way. Cody notices things other people miss, a scuffed boot, a missing ribbon, a friend having a hard week, and he can’t leave well enough alone once he has. He keeps a notebook on him at all times, just in case.

Australian Shepherd · Cody’s Shadow

Koda

Cody’s dog and constant companion. One blue eye, one brown eye, and a copper-and-white coat that turns heads at the county fair. Her tail goes like a helicopter blade when she’s happy, and she presses in close when something’s wrong. Where Cody goes, Koda goes.

The Barn Cat · Sees Everything

Nimbus

The Briggs family’s barn cat. Nobody remembers exactly when she showed up, only that she runs the barn now. Nimbus keeps her own hours, chooses her own people, and misses nothing that happens on the farm. When something goes missing, she’s usually the reason, and usually for a good one.

Cody’s Dad

Tony Briggs

Runs the farm, steady and quiet about it. Proud of Cody in the way that shows up more in what he does than what he says.

Cody’s Mom

Amie Briggs

Runs the flower shop in town. Warm, watchful, and good at knowing when someone needs a minute before they need words.

Cody’s Big Sister

Maddie Briggs

Fourteen years old. Eye-rolling, but not unkind, and she notices more of what happens on the farm than she lets on.
